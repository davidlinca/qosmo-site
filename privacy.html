Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

QOSMO is operated by DAVILEX SOFT S.R.L., a company registered in Romania ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact: david@qosmoapp.com

This policy explains what QOSMO collects, why, who it is shared with, and what control you have over it. We have tried to write it plainly rather than defensively, because you are trusting us with information about something private and often difficult.

1. The short version

  • Your recovery data — streaks, urges, relapses, journal entries — is private to your account. Nobody else using QOSMO can see it. Only what you post in the community is visible to others, under a generated handle, never your name.
  • We ask for your explicit consent before the onboarding questions. Most of your answers are used on your phone to build your plan and are not stored.
  • We never see your browsing history. The content blocker runs entirely on your device through Apple's Screen Time system. We cannot see which sites you visit, which sites are blocked, or which apps you choose to block.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising or use advertising identifiers. The only analytics in the app concern subscriptions and our subscription screens (section 2.7).
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time. That erases your data, including your community posts. The few records that remain are listed in section 6.

2.2 Onboarding questions

Before the onboarding questions start, QOSMO asks for your explicit consent (section 3).

The questions cover things like how often you watch porn, how long sessions last, whether it has escalated, how old you were when you were first exposed, your mood, your triggers, which parts of your life it affects, previous attempts to quit, and how you heard about QOSMO. You also pick symptoms and goals, and make a vow.

These answers are used on your device during onboarding to build your plan, and are not stored. The only things saved to your account from onboarding are:

  • the name you entered
  • your age and gender
  • the goals you choose to track
  • that you completed onboarding

2.3 Profile information

Age, gender, and the recovery goals you choose to track. Optionally, if you add them: sexual orientation (in Edit Profile) and a religious content preference (in Edit Profile or when choosing prayer content), which is used to select prayer content.

Profile photo (optional): the photo stays on your device. We store only a reference to where it is saved on your phone. The image itself is never uploaded.

2.4 Recovery information

This is the core of the app and the most sensitive data we hold:

  • Streak start date, current streak, best streak
  • Relapse logs — date, and the trigger, time of day, feeling and goal you record
  • Urge logs — date, intensity, triggers, the kind of place you were (for example "bedroom" or "work" — never your actual location), whether you were alone, and how you responded. Urges you ride out through a game on the panic screen are logged automatically, with the game's name
  • Daily pledges (when you made them) and which daily protocol tasks you completed
  • Journal entries — title, text and tags, in your own words. If you add a photo to an entry, it stays on your device; only a reference to it is stored
  • Lesson progress

2.5 Community information

Posts (title, text and tags), comments, likes, views, follows, blocks and reports.

  • Anything you post or comment is visible to every other signed-in QOSMO user. It carries your generated handle — never your real name, and never your email. Links cannot be posted.
  • Likes are part of the community and are not private to you.
  • When you open a post, a view is recorded so its author can see a view count.
  • Who you follow and who you block are private to you.
  • If you report a post or comment, we store the reason you chose, a copy of up to 500 characters of the reported text, and which accounts filed and wrote it. Reports are not visible to other users, including the person reported.
  • Before your first post or comment you accept the community rules, and we store when you did.

You can swap your handle for a new generated one in Edit Profile, though posts you have already made keep the handle they were published under. Old handles stay reserved to your account, so nobody else can take one and pose as you.

Please do not post anything you would not want another user to read, and avoid including details that would identify you.

2.6 Device and technical information

Reminders — the daily protocol reminder at 10 AM, and the reminder that your next pledge is ready 24 hours after your last one (moved to 8 AM if it would fall overnight) — are scheduled on your device.

If you allow notifications, we also store a push notification token with your platform, device model and iOS version. The token is issued through Expo's push service and Apple, so that we can send notifications from our servers in future. QOSMO does not currently send any. We do not store your device's name.

Like any online service, the providers in section 5 receive your device's IP address when the app connects to them.

2.7 Subscription information

  • RevenueCat manages your subscription. It receives your QOSMO account identifier, your purchase and subscription history from Apple, and device information including Apple's identifier for vendor.
  • Superwall displays our subscription screens. It receives your QOSMO account identifier; device and app information such as device model, iOS version, language, region, time zone, app version, install date and Apple's identifier for vendor; your subscription status and purchases; and events about those screens — which were shown, when, and whether you closed them, subscribed or restored a purchase. Superwall uses this to measure and target our subscription screens. It does not receive your onboarding answers, profile, recovery or community data, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your card details.

2.8 What we deliberately do not collect

  • Browsing history. The blocker applies Apple's own adult-content filter via the Screen Time system. Filtering happens on your device and Apple does not report back to us what was blocked or visited.
  • Which apps or websites you block. Apple's picker returns opaque identifiers that only Apple's own frameworks can interpret. Your selection never leaves your device and is meaningless to us.
  • Camera images. The panic screens show a live view from your front camera behind the on-screen content. Nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Your photos. Profile and journal photos stay on your device (sections 2.3 and 2.4).
  • Most onboarding answers (section 2.2).
  • Tracking. No advertising identifier, no advertising tools, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no general analytics or crash-reporting tools. The subscription analytics described in section 2.7 are the only analytics in the app.

3. Special category data and your consent

Some of what QOSMO holds is "special category" data under Article 9 of the GDPR, which carries stronger protection:

  • Data concerning your sex life and sexual health — urge and relapse logs, pledges and streaks are inherently about sexual behaviour, and some goals you may track (for example, improved libido and sex life) concern sexual health. The onboarding answers used on your device are of the same kind
  • Sexual orientation — if you choose to provide it
  • Religious beliefs — if you choose a religious content preference

We process this on the basis of your explicit consent.

How you give it. Before the onboarding questions, QOSMO shows a screen explaining what we will ask about, why, and who can see it, with an unticked box: "I agree to QOSMO processing this information, including health-related data, to provide the app." You cannot continue until you tick it, and if you choose "Not now" the questions are not asked. We store a record that you agreed, which version of that wording you saw, and when.

Withdrawing it. You may withdraw consent at any time. QOSMO cannot work without this information, so withdrawing means deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) or emailing david@qosmoapp.com and asking us to delete it. You can also delete individual journal entries, and set orientation to "Prefer not to say". Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew.

Orientation and religious preference are optional. You can use QOSMO fully without providing either.

6. How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. If you never sign in, your anonymous account holds only a placeholder profile and, if you gave it, your consent record; it is kept until you delete it in Settings or ask us to.

Deleting your account. Settings → Delete account (also available in Edit Profile and on the subscription screen) permanently deletes:

  • your profile — name, email, age, gender, orientation, religious preference, tracked goals, streaks, handle and consent record
  • your urge and relapse logs, journal entries, pledges, protocol history and lesson progress
  • your block list, the people you follow, and your push tokens
  • your community posts, with the comments, likes and views on them, and your comments on other people's posts
  • then your sign-in account itself. If you use Sign in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke QOSMO's access to your Apple ID.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings (Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions), or you will keep being charged.

What remains after deletion:

  • Your reserved handles — each handle you held and the identifier of the deleted account, kept so nobody can reuse a handle to impersonate you
  • Reports you filed, or that concern your posts or comments, including the reason and text snapshot — kept for moderation for 12 months
  • Likes and views you left on other people's posts
  • Records held by RevenueCat and Superwall about your purchases and subscription screens. Email david@qosmoapp.com and we will have them deleted, except records that must be kept by law (for example for tax)

We do not keep backups of the database. Google retains earlier versions of stored data for up to one hour for technical reasons, after which deleted data is permanently gone.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, or object to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time (section 3).

In the app, Settings → Delete account erases your data immediately, and Edit Profile lets you correct your profile. There is no in-app export: to get a copy of your data, or for anything else, email david@qosmoapp.com. We will respond within one month, and send your data in a machine-readable format (JSON).

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you may complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro), or to the authority in your own country.

8. Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase and protected by server-side security rules that restrict your private data to your own signed-in account, and community content to signed-in users. We test these rules. Access is authenticated, and data is encrypted in transit and, by Google, at rest. QOSMO does not upload files: your photos stay on your device.

Access to the database is limited to one person at DAVILEX SOFT S.R.L., and is used only to act on a report, answer a support or data request, or fix a technical problem.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the relevant authority as required.

9. Minimum age

You must be at least 16 to use QOSMO. The app asks your age and does not accept anyone younger. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has provided us with data, contact david@qosmoapp.com and we will delete it.
