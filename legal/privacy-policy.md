# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 28 August 2026

QOSMO is operated by **DAVILEX SOFT S.R.L.**, a company registered in Romania
("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

**Contact:** david@qosmoapp.com

This policy explains what QOSMO collects, why, who it is shared with, and what
control you have over it. We have tried to write it plainly rather than
defensively, because you are trusting us with information about something
private and often difficult.

---

## 1. The short version

- Your recovery data — streaks, urges, relapses, journal entries — is private to
  your account. Nobody else using QOSMO can see it.
- **We never see your browsing history.** The content blocker runs entirely on
  your device through Apple's Screen Time system. We cannot see which sites you
  visit, which sites are blocked, or which apps you choose to block.
- We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising.
- You can delete your account and all of it from inside the app, at any time.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account information

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| An anonymous account identifier | Created when you first open QOSMO, so your data has somewhere to live before you sign in |
| Email address | If you sign in with email, Apple or Google |
| Name | Taken from your onboarding answers. Shown to you inside the app only — **never** beside your community posts |
| A community handle | A name we generate for you, such as "QuietFox42". This, and only this, is what other users see |

If you sign in with Apple and use Hide My Email, we only ever receive Apple's
relay address, not your real one.

### 2.2 Profile information

Age, gender, sexual orientation (optional), religious content preference
(optional, used to select prayer content), profile photo (optional), and the
recovery goals you choose to track.

### 2.3 Recovery information

This is the core of the app and the most sensitive data we hold:

- Streak start date, current streak, best streak
- Relapse logs — date, and the trigger, time of day, feeling and goal you record
- Urge logs — intensity, trigger, whether you were alone, how you responded
- Daily pledges and daily protocol completion
- Journal entries, in your own words
- Lesson and quiz progress

### 2.4 Community information

Posts, comments, likes, views and follows. **Anything you post in the community
is visible to every other signed-in QOSMO user.**

Posts and comments carry a handle we generate for you — never your real name,
and never your email. You can change your handle at any time in Edit Profile,
though posts you have already made keep the handle they were published under.

Please do not post anything you would not want another user to read, and avoid
including details that would identify you.

### 2.5 Device and technical information

A push notification token, your platform, device name and model — used only to
deliver notifications you have enabled.

### 2.6 Subscription information

Your subscription status and purchase history, through RevenueCat. **Payment is
handled entirely by Apple.** We never receive or store your card details.

### 2.7 What we deliberately do not collect

- **Browsing history.** The blocker applies Apple's own adult-content filter via
  the Screen Time system. Filtering happens on your device and Apple does not
  report back to us what was blocked or visited.
- **Which apps you block.** Apple's picker returns opaque identifiers that only
  Apple's own frameworks can interpret. Your selection never leaves your device
  and is meaningless to us.
- **Camera images.** The panic screen may use your front camera to produce a
  blurred background effect. Nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted.

## 3. Special category data

Some of what QOSMO holds is "special category" data under Article 9 of the GDPR,
which carries stronger protection:

- **Data concerning your sex life** — urge and relapse logs are inherently about
  sexual behaviour
- **Sexual orientation** — if you choose to provide it
- **Religious beliefs** — if you choose a religious content preference

We process this on the basis of your **explicit consent**, given when you create
an account and record this information. You may withdraw consent at any time by
deleting the relevant entries or your entire account. Withdrawal does not affect
processing carried out before you withdrew.

Orientation and religious preference are optional. You can use QOSMO fully
without providing either.

## 5. Who your data is shared with

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We use these processors:

| Processor | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Google (Firebase) | Authentication and database | EU / US |
| RevenueCat | Subscription management | US |
| Superwall | Displaying subscription offers | US |
| Expo | Delivering push notifications | US |
| Apple | Sign-in and payment processing | US / EU |

Transfers outside the EEA rely on the European Commission's Standard
Contractual Clauses.

We may also disclose data where legally required, or where necessary to protect
someone's life or safety.

---

## 6. How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account removes your
profile, streaks, urge and relapse logs, journal entries, pledges, protocol
history, lesson progress and push tokens.

Two things to be aware of:

- **Community posts and comments may remain visible** after account deletion, as
  they form part of other users' conversations. Delete them individually first
  if you want them gone.
- Backups may retain copies for up to 30 days before being overwritten.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, erasure,
restriction of processing, portability, or object to processing. You may
withdraw consent at any time.

The fastest route for most of these is in the app: **Settings → Delete account**
removes your data immediately. For anything else, email david@qosmoapp.com. We
will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you may complain to the Romanian
supervisory authority, **ANSPDCP** (www.dataprotection.ro), or to the authority
in your own country.

---

## 8. Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase and protected by server-side security rules
that restrict every user's data to that user alone. Access is authenticated, and
transfers are encrypted in transit.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach
affects your data we will notify you and the relevant authority as required.

---

## 9. Children

QOSMO is intended for adults and is rated 17+ on the App Store. It is not for
anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe
a child has provided us with data, contact david@qosmoapp.com and we will delete
it.
